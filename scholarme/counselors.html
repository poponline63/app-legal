How the counselor view works

The student taps Share in their pipeline and texts you the link. You open it in any browser.

  1. Student saves scholarships they plan to chase
  2. They tap Share and send you the private link
  3. You see names, amounts, statuses, and deadlines
  4. You coach. They apply. Nobody misses a date.
